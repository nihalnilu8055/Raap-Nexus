Frequently asked questions

Short answers about data, cost, models and limits.

Does my data leave my computer?

The app, database and settings stay on your computer. What the agent fetches from a connector to answer a question goes to the AI model you chose, because that is how it can answer. Local models (Ollama) keep even that on your computer, and Mask personal details replaces identifying values before they go to a model.

Can the AI change my accounting data?

No by default. Connectors read. A tool that could change data stops and asks you first (Read-only and approvals), and automatic workflow runs are read-only.

Which AI should I use?

For questions about your data, an agent that can use connectors: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, or a URL model with tool calling. Ollama is a good private, free helper for Team mode. Try two and compare (AI agents).

What does it cost?

Raap Nexus itself starts on a free plan (Your plan). The AI you plug in has its own cost, for example an API bill or a subscription. Raap Nexus is built to spend fewer tokens: skills are chosen per question, knowledge is searched instead of sent, saved reports are reused, and Team mode gives each agent only what it needs.

How do I save tokens?

  • Ask specific questions with a period and a filter.
  • Switch on only the skills you use, and give each agent only what it needs.
  • Let an agent study your systems once (Knowledge).
  • Keep saved reports on (Saved reports).
  • Use workflow steps that need no AI ("Call a connector tool", "Make a file").

Can several people use it?

Yes. Add users with their own sign-ins and permissions, and share dashboards with a role (Users and permissions). Your plan sets how many.

Does it work offline?

The app and the help work offline. Agents that call an online model, voice typing, notifications and registration need an internet connection. A local model with a folder or SQLite connector works fully offline.

Which languages are available?

18: English, Arabic, Urdu, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Turkish, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam, Indonesian, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese and Korean (Languages and appearance).

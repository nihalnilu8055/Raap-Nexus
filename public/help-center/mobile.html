Use Raap Nexus on your phone

Open it in a phone browser, install it as an app, or use the Android app. The data stays on your computer.

How it works

Raap Nexus always runs on your computer. Your phone is a window onto it, so the computer must be on and reachable. There are three ways to reach it:

WayGood for
Phone modeQuick access from anywhere with a secure tunnel, no router settings (Domain, port and phone mode).
Your own domainA permanent address that works for the Android app, shared dashboards and approval links.
Wi-FiStart Raap Nexus (phone access) from the Start menu for the same network.

Install it as an app

A small card at the bottom of the screen offers to install Raap Nexus as an app (Chrome and Edge from their own button). On an iPhone, open it in Safari, choose Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open it from there. Choose Don't ask again to hide the card.

The Android app

  1. Install the .apk from Techlogica.
  2. Give it the address of your computer: your domain, or the phone-mode link.
  3. Sign in with your normal username and password.
  4. For alerts, switch them on in Settings > Account > Notifications inside the app: it shows each alert as a notification, with no Google account or push service involved. Allow Raap Nexus to run in the background if alerts arrive late.

On a phone, Raap Nexus opens on your dashboard first, and the layout adapts to the narrow screen.

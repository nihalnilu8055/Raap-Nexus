Languages and appearance

18 interface languages, light and dark themes and an accent colour, chosen per browser.

Choose a language

Raap Nexus speaks 18 languages: English, Arabic, Urdu, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Turkish, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam, Indonesian, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese and Korean. Arabic and Urdu mirror the whole layout from right to left.

  1. On the sign-in screen use the language list at the top of the card, or
  2. click the globe button at the bottom of the sidebar, or
  3. open Settings > System > General and use Language.

The choice is saved in this browser. Each person, and each browser, can use a different language. Texts without a translation yet stay in English, and translations are machine-quality until a native speaker has reviewed them.

Theme and accent colour

Under Settings > System > General > Appearance choose System, Light or Dark, and one of four accent colours. The moon button in the sidebar switches quickly.

This help centre

The language list at the top of every help page changes the text, the screenshots and the button names to match your language. The translations are machine-made and not yet reviewed by a native speaker; the English text is the reference.

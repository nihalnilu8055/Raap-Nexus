Create your workspace and sign in

The first visit creates the owner login. Then you pick your language, finish the Get started list and save your recovery code.

Create the owner login

The very first time you open Raap Nexus it asks you to create the login for this workspace.

  1. Open http://localhost:8787 (the installer does this for you).
  2. Choose your language from the list at the top of the card. All 18 languages are available (Languages and appearance).
  3. Type a Username, a Password and the same password again, then press Create account & start.

The person who creates the first login is the owner: every permission, always. The owner adds everyone else later (Users and permissions).

Careful

The owner login cannot be recovered by email. Choose a strong password you will remember, and save the recovery code described below.

Get started

A short checklist waits on the chat screen: connect your data, choose an AI agent, ask your first question, and keep it in front of you (a dashboard). Each step ticks itself when you do it. Press Hide this when you no longer need it.

Save your recovery code

Raap Nexus encrypts its data and ties it to this computer. If you ever move to a new computer or change hardware, the recovery code is what lets you open your data again. The first time you sign in, a red banner reminds you. Open Settings > System > Data protection and press Show my recovery code, then print it or write it down and keep it away from this computer (Data protection and sign-in security).

Signing in later

Use the same address and your username and password. If your administrator turned on a second step, you also type a 6-digit code from your authenticator app.

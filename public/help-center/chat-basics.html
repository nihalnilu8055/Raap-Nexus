Ask your data a question

Type a question in plain words. The agent looks it up in your connectors and answers, often with a table.

Ask

  1. Open the chat (New chat).
  2. Check the box under the question field: the dashboard it is about, the model that answers, and (if you want) Team.
  3. Type your question and press Enter, for example "Which customers have overdue invoices?"

While the agent works you see what it is doing, step by step, and can press the stop button at any time. Everything it fetches from a connector is shown in the answer so you can check it.

Good questions

  • Be specific about the period, company and filter: "Receivables ageing for March, top 10 overdue customers".
  • Words like "this month", "yesterday" and "last week" are worked out when you ask.
  • Say "pull a new report" to force fresh data instead of a saved copy (Saved reports).

Handling your answers

  • Say "add this to the dashboard" and the answer becomes a widget (Pin answers to a dashboard).
  • Copy a table or the whole answer with the buttons under it.
  • Earlier chats are listed in the sidebar by day; search them with Search chats. Clear chats deletes many at once (a chat that is still answering is left alone, and deleted chats cannot be brought back).

Voice typing

Press the microphone and speak. The browser sends your speech to its own speech service, so it needs an internet connection, and your browser must be allowed to use the microphone for this site.

Tip

Always check important figures. Answers come from your connectors through the agent you picked, and an AI can still misread a question.

Approvals

Multi-level approvals with signatures. The workflow pauses until everyone has decided, and a signed record is kept.

How it works

An approval step asks people to approve a request: an amount, a customer, any record. Levels run in order, and a level whose rule is false is skipped. The workflow waits until everyone who must decide has decided.

  1. Start from the Multi-level approval template under Automations > Templates, or add an Ask for approval step to a workflow.
  2. Name each level and list its approvers, one per line: a name, an email and/or WhatsApp number, @username for one Raap Nexus user, or level:2 for everyone at approval level 2 (give people a level under Settings > People).
  3. Write a rule for when a level applies, for example number(trigger.body.amount) >= 10000. Leave it empty to always ask.
  4. Choose who is emailed the signed record.

What approvers see

Each approver gets a private link by email (or you can send it on WhatsApp from the Approvals tab). They review the request, then approve or decline. By approving they confirm the request: their name, signature, the time and their network address are recorded together with a fingerprint of the request.

A person with an earlier level still to decide cannot decide yet; they are told to open the link again later. Once a request is closed, nothing more is needed.

The Approvals tab

Automations > Approvals lists requests waiting for approvers and the signed record of the finished ones. You can remind an approver or cancel a request (approvers can then no longer decide it).

Note

If approvers are outside this computer or network, set a public address or use phone mode so their links open (Domain, port and phone mode).

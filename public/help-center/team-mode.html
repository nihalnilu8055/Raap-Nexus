Team mode and the team split

Let the lead model split a request into parts and hand them to your other agents, in parallel.

What it does

With Team on, the model you picked becomes the lead. When a request has several independent parts (data pulls, per-company or per-period work, a second opinion on important numbers) it sends them to your other agents at the same time, checks what comes back and gives you one answer.

Turn it on

  1. Switch on at least two agents (AI agents).
  2. In the chat box press Team. It turns to Team: on. With fewer than two agents it tells you what to add.
  3. Ask your question as usual.

How a request is split

The split is worked out on your computer, with no model call, so the lead does not spend tokens planning:

  1. A numbered or bulleted list: one part per item.
  2. A fan-out over entities: "for the companies Alpha, Beta and Gamma" gives one part per company.
  3. Separate actions joined by "and also", "as well as", a semicolon, or "and" before a new action: "reconcile the debtors and check the stock ageing".

It does not split when one part depends on another ("then compare it with that result"), when a comparison needs both sides ("compare January and February"), or when the parts need the same skill. There are at most five parts. Each part gets only the one or two skills it needs, and the least busy agent that can reach your data is chosen.

Tip

Write a request with several parts as a list, or name the entities, to get the best split.

Together with masking

If Mask personal details is on, every agent in the team works on masked values, and the team card shows you the real ones.

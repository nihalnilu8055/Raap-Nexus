What is Raap Nexus

A private AI workspace that answers from your own systems, builds dashboards and runs approvals and automations on your computer.

The idea in one minute

Raap Nexus runs on your computer. You ask a question in plain words, and an AI agent looks the answer up in your systems (your ERP, a database, a folder of files) and writes it back, often with a table or a chart. Anything worth keeping can be pinned to a dashboard that refreshes by itself.

How your data is treated

  • Your data stays on this computer. The app, its database and your settings live in a data folder on your machine, encrypted and tied to it.
  • Read-only by default. Connectors only read. A tool that could create, change or delete something stops and asks you to Allow or Deny first (read more).
  • You choose the AI. Use an AI command-line tool already installed on your computer (Claude, Codex, Gemini, Ollama and others), or any model reachable by a web address and key.

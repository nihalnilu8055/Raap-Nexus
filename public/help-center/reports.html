Reports

Reports made of blocks: tables, totals, figures and charts. Describe one once in Chat, then make it for any period as a page, PDF, Excel and Word file, with no AI.

What a report is

A report is a list of blocks: headings, text, tables (grouped, with subtotals and a total), figures such as a total or a count, and charts. Describe it once in Chat, for example “a monthly sales report by branch with a chart”. After that Raap Nexus makes it again by code, for any period, with no AI and no tokens, so the figures are exactly your system's.

Make one

  1. Ask in Chat for the report you want. It is saved with its blocks, ready to be made again.
  2. Open Settings > Documents, find the report under Reports, choose a period (This month, Last month, This quarter, Year to date and more) and press Make.
  3. Open the page, or download it as PDF, Excel or Word. All four show the same figures.

Change it from Chat

Say what to change: “group by branch”, “add a chart of the top 5 customers”, “move the totals to the top”, “show last quarter”. Each change is a small edit to the list of blocks, so it costs very little. You can also use the arrows, Remove, Group by, subtotals and total on the Reports card.

Several fields to find a record

A document or report can ask for several values to find exactly one record, for example the invoice number and the branch. A value can be optional, and one value can match either of two fields (a number or a reference). If several records match, Raap Nexus lists them and tells you what to add.

Send it automatically

Add the step Make a report document to a workflow, choose the report and the period (for example last month), then add an email step and attach the files. On a monthly schedule the pack arrives on its own (Workflows and templates).

A report for each customer

Install the template Overdue invoices emailed to each customer from the workflow templates. Choose the connectors to read, and an AI agent learns the reading once. After that, code splits the overdue invoices by customer and emails each customer only their own invoices with the total, to the email address held in your system. It starts in test mode, so every mail comes to you first; tick Send to the customers only when the test mails look right.

What uses AI

Only describing a report the first time. Making it, changing a block, choosing another period and drawing the charts are done by code. For flagged exceptions in a table, see Less AI, fewer tokens.

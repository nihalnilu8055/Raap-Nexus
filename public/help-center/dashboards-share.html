Share a dashboard, comments and refresh

Four roles decide what each person can do. Refresh re-pulls the reports behind your charts.

Share

On a dashboard press Share (for the dashboard's admins). Pick a person and a role:

RoleCan
ViewerSee the dashboard and its widgets.
CommentatorAlso comment on the dashboard and widgets, and chat about it.
EditorAlso add, change, move and delete widgets and tabs (also through chat).
AdminAlso rename or delete the dashboard and manage who it is shared with.

The owner and a dashboard's creator are always admin. People without access do not see the dashboard at all. To chat, a person needs the chat permission and at least Commentator on a dashboard (Users and permissions).

Share a link

The Share window can also give you a link for a shared dashboard. The link uses your domain if you set one, otherwise the running phone-mode address, otherwise your network address (Domain, port and phone mode).

Refresh data

Refresh data makes an agent pull the same reports again: only entries that are posted or confirmed, continuing from the date each chart last covered, and rebuilding a chart from the start only when its opening or closing balance changed. Widgets built before this feature existed may ask you to rebuild them once.

Comments

Commentators and above can write comments; a Viewer can read them. Comments are listed on the dashboard and on each widget.

Dashboard settings and saved reports

Choose which connectors each dashboard may use, and how saved reports are reused.

Dashboards page

Open Settings > Data > Dashboards. Each dashboard has its own tabs and its own connectors. In chat you pick which dashboard you are working on, and the AI can only use that dashboard's connectors and adds widgets only to it.

  1. Press New dashboard (or use an existing one) and give it a name.
  2. Choose which connectors it may use. Dashboards made by people without settings access start with no connectors until an administrator adds them.
  3. Keep at least one dashboard: the last one cannot be deleted. Deleting a dashboard moves its chats to your first one.

Saved reports

Reports you pull are kept encrypted on this computer, so the same report is not pulled again and again. Under Settings > System > General > Saved reports:

  • When a saved copy exists: ask once per question (recommended), reuse it, or always pull a new one.
  • Reuse without asking if it is newer than: always ask, 10 minutes, 30 minutes or 1 hour.
  • Keep saved reports for: 1, 7 or 30 days.

Reports for the current month, current year or today are always pulled fresh.

Custom instructions

Under the same General page, Custom instructions are added to every new conversation. Good for business context, currency and fiscal calendar, for example: Always answer in English. Our financial year starts in April. Amounts are in AED. Prefer tables.

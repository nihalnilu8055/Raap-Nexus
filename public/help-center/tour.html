A tour of the screen

The sidebar, the chat box and where each setting lives.

The sidebar

ItemWhat it does
New chatStarts a fresh conversation.
Your dashboardsOne entry per dashboard you may see. Open it to see its tabs and widgets (Dashboards).
AutomationsWorkflows, templates, approvals and alerts (Automations).
SettingsEverything you configure, in six groups: AI, Data, Customers, People, System and Account.
Search chatsFinds an earlier conversation. Your chats are listed by day below it.

At the bottom of the sidebar, from the left: your name, Phone mode, the language button, dark or light, and sign out. The small button at the top collapses the sidebar.

The chat box

Below the question field you choose what the question is about and who answers it:

  • Dashboard: the dashboard this chat is about. Only that dashboard's connectors are used, and widgets are added to it.
  • Connectors: shows how many connectors the chat can reach.
  • Team: lets the lead model split work across your other agents (Team mode).
  • Model: the agent and model that answers.
  • The microphone for voice typing, and the send arrow.

Type / to pick a skill for a single message (Skills in chat).

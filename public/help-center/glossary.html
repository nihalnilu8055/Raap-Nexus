Glossary

The words used in Raap Nexus and this help.

WordMeaning
AgentAn AI you plug in: a CLI on your computer or a model by URL and key.
CLIA command-line program, here an AI tool such as Claude Code, Codex or Gemini.
ConnectorHow the AI reaches a system: an ERP, database, folder or API. Read-only.
MCPModel Context Protocol, a standard way for AI tools to call tools and read data.
DashboardA page of widgets, in tabs, that you build by asking the AI.
WidgetOne number, chart, table or map on a dashboard.
SkillAn instruction pack the assistant loads only when a question needs it.
GuardrailA rule an agent must follow, with optional checks on what it writes.
Team modeThe lead model splits work across several agents in parallel.
WorkflowSteps that run on a schedule or a trigger.
ApprovalA workflow step where named people must sign off, with a signed record.
KnowledgeVerified facts the agents learned about your systems, searched when needed.
PlaceholderA stand-in such as Customer_0001 that replaces a real name when masking is on.
Phone modeA secure tunnel that lets your phone reach this computer.
Recovery codeThe code that opens your data on another computer or after a hardware change.
OwnerThe first user, with every permission, always.
TechlogicaThe company that develops Raap Nexus, issues keys and connectors, and delivers updates.
MarketplaceWhere you ask Techlogica for more users, dashboards, products and add-ons.
Site and group hubA company location running Raap Nexus, and the hub that shows several sites together.

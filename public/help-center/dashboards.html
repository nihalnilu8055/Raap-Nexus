Dashboards, tabs and widgets

An animated dashboard with tabs, per-widget period buttons, maps and 15+ chart types, built by asking the AI.

The dashboard

Open a dashboard from the sidebar. At the top you see its name, a summary strip of headline findings, and the actions. Below, the tabs group related widgets, and a + adds a tab.

ButtonWhat it does
DashboardsSwitch to another dashboard.
Ask in chatOpen a chat about this dashboard.
CommentsRead and write comments on the dashboard and on widgets.
Refresh dataPull the reports behind the charts again (details).
ShareGive people access (Share a dashboard).
PresentFull-screen presentation mode.

Widgets

Widgets show a number, a chart, a table or a map. Each can have its own period buttons (for example Quarter) so one widget can show a different range from its neighbour. There are more than 15 chart types, including gauges, trend lines, rankings and maps.

Build by asking

You rarely build a widget by hand. Ask in chat and say "add it to the dashboard". Drag widgets to reorder them; hover for the menu to rename or remove one.

What-if and dashboard tools

  • A period selector re-states the widgets for a month, quarter, year or custom range.
  • A what-if panel lets you change an assumption and see the effect on the figures.
  • Maps colour regions by a value, with the places taken from the data.

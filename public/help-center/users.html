Users and permissions

Add people, choose what each may do, and share dashboards with them. Everyone signs in with their own username and password.

The owner and everyone else

The person who first signs up is the owner: every permission, always, and that cannot be changed. The owner (or anyone given Manage users) adds people under Settings > People.

Add a person

  1. Press Add user.
  2. Enter their name, username, email and phone, and a password (at least 8 characters, not a common one).
  3. Choose a permission set (below), or Custom to tick each permission.
  4. Pick the dashboards this person may see, and optionally an approval level so they can sign approvals (Approvals).

Permission sets

SetFor
AdministratorEverything except managing users.
AnalystChat, their own dashboards, and seeing automations.
ApproverSigns approvals and opens dashboards shared with them. No chat.
ViewerOnly opens dashboards shared with them.
CustomChoose each permission yourself.

The individual permissions

PermissionAllows
Use chat and agentsAsk questions about dashboards they have access to (commentator or higher).
Create their own dashboardsMake new dashboards and share them (they are admin of those).
See automationsView workflows, run history and alerts.
Manage automationsCreate, edit and run workflows; email settings; see every approval.
Manage agents, connectors and settingsAgents, skills, connectors, phone mode and general settings. This includes connecting programs on this computer, so give it only to people you trust.
Manage usersCreate users and change their permissions. Give this only to people you trust fully.

Looking after people

  • Lost phone and recovery codes: open the person's edit window and switch their second step off. They are signed out and can sign in again with the password, or must set it up again if the owner requires it.
  • Removing someone: they lose access, their chats are deleted and their dashboards go back to the owner.
  • To chat, a person needs the chat permission and at least Commentator on a dashboard; the dashboard decides which connectors the chat can use.

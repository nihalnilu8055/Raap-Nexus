What is shared with Techlogica

Nothing about your business data is shared. Two optional, off-by-default switches share how your software works and how skills are chosen.

The two switches

Both are on the Techlogica page, both are off by default, and you can turn either off at any time (what was already shared stays with Techlogica).

SwitchWhat is sharedWhat is never shared
What the assistant learns about your softwareNotes on how the software works: what a table means, which report answers what. If Techlogica has no profile yet, the names of its tables and columns.Customers, amounts, numbers, rows, host names, passwords. They are refused and stay on this computer.
Share skill-choosing trainingA skill's name, one word stem and three counts. In return you receive what other installations learned.Your questions, documents, figures, names, and skills you wrote.

Both show you exactly what would be sent before you switch them on, and you can delete individual learnings. See How the assistant chooses skills for the second.

Besides these

  • Registering sends your company name, email and software so a key can be issued.
  • Plan checks and update checks contact Techlogica.
  • The diagnostics file (Health and support) is shown to you and only sent if you send it.

Your AI model provider (for a URL agent) receives what you ask and the data the agent fetches to answer it; use Mask personal details to limit what identifies people in that.
